# Aethel Fold

## Short description

Fold the pages of a pop-up book to crush a paper army, rip its castle apart and fold a raging origami dragon into a frog.

## Description

A paper army marches across a pop-up storybook, and you are the hand that folds it. Swipe the dotted creases to raise walls, snap ravines shut on marching knights, fling catapults back with a flap and tear the castle towers flat. Six hand-crafted pages build from a border skirmish to a siege, then the castle itself unfolds into an origami dragon. Break its creases, make the last fold, and watch it become a very confused frog.

## How to play

1. **Protect the little hero** at the bottom of the page. Every soldier who reaches the hero costs a heart; lose all three and the page crumples, and you replay it from the start.
2. **Follow the dotted arrows.** Every glowing crease on the page is a fold you can make. Swipe along its arrow and the paper follows your finger. Let go past halfway and it snaps shut.
   - **Walls and shields** pop up in front of the enemy and block marching soldiers and arrows.
   - **Ravines** fold shut on anyone standing on them. Tap a closed ravine to **stamp** it flat for bonus points.
   - **Launch flaps** flip catapult crews and their boulders right back where they came from.
   - **Hills** fold up to close a road, so the army has to march the long way round.
3. **Rip the castle apart.** Glowing tower creases can be torn. Pull the paper apart and the tower comes down.
4. **Peel the page.** Cleared pages turn by themselves, except after the castle gates: there you drag the dog-eared corner to peel back the paper and find what is hiding underneath.
5. **Fold the dragon.** On the castle core the fortress unfolds into an origami dragon. Fold its legs and wings back along their glowing creases, tear open its core, then make the last fold.
6. **Score big.** Crushing several soldiers with one fold builds a combo, and clearing a page without losing a heart is a *Perfect Page*.

You never need to read a manual: the first time each fold appears, time slows down and a paper hand shows you the move.

## Controls

| Action | Touch | Mouse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fold (walls, ravines, flaps, hills, dragon creases) | Swipe along the dotted arrow | Click and drag along the dotted arrow |
| Stamp a closed ravine | Tap it | Click it |
| Tear a glowing crease | Spread two fingers on it (or drag across it) | Drag across it, or scroll the wheel over it |
| Peel the page (after the castle gates) | Drag the dog-eared corner | Drag the dog-eared corner |
| Pause / settings | Paper-gear button (top right) | Paper-gear button, **Esc** or **P** |
| Mute | Speaker button (top right) | Speaker button |
| Play again after victory | *Play again* button | *Play again* button or **Enter** |

Your progress (current page, score, best records, settings) is saved automatically, and you pick up on the page you left.
